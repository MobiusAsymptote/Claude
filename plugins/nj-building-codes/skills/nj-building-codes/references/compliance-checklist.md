# Compliance Checklist and Common Pitfalls

## Project Phase Compliance Checklist

### Pre-Application / Entitlement Phase

**Zoning and Land Use**
- [ ] Verify municipal zoning permits residential use at proposed density
- [ ] Confirm inclusionary zoning requirements for the municipality (municipal FAIR SHARE PLAN)
- [ ] Identify municipal affordable housing obligation tier (Round 2 certified, pending Fourth Round, court-ordered, etc.)
- [ ] Confirm required percentage of affordable units (typically 10–20% in inclusionary zone)
- [ ] Determine required bedroom distribution per municipal FAIR SHARE PLAN and UHAC
- [ ] Verify whether COAH/court-order substantive certification is in place (affects which rules apply)

**Environmental and Site**
- [ ] Phase I ESA completed
- [ ] Flood zone determination (FEMA); NJDEP approval if in floodplain
- [ ] DEP freshwater wetlands delineation if applicable
- [ ] Stormwater management pre-application meeting with municipality (RSIS N.J.A.C. 5:21-7)
- [ ] NJPDES permit required if ≥1 acre disturbed

**Funding Application**
- [ ] Identify required funding sources (LIHTC, HOME, AHPF, AHGS, municipal trust fund)
- [ ] NJHMFA LIHTC application: confirm current QAP cycle and deadlines
- [ ] Developer training certificate obtained (required for NJHMFA)
- [ ] ENERGY STAR pathway identified and HERS Rater engaged early

---

### Schematic Design / SD Phase

**Accessibility**
- [ ] Confirm whether FHA design requirements apply (4+ units?)
- [ ] Confirm whether Section 504 applies (federal funding source?)
- [ ] Calculate Section 504 unit counts: 5% mobility (round up) + 2% sensory (round up)
- [ ] Locate Type A units and sensory-accessible units on floor plans
- [ ] Confirm all ground-floor units or elevator-served units meet FHA 7 requirements (Type B minimum)
- [ ] Review door widths: 32″ clear minimum at all covered unit doors and common areas
- [ ] Identify accessible parking stalls: 1 van-accessible per 25 total spaces
- [ ] Accessible route from parking to building entrance to units (continuous, no steps)

**UHAC Affordability Controls**
- [ ] Confirm unit count and bedroom mix matches UHAC and FAIR SHARE PLAN requirements
- [ ] Identify Administrative Agent (must be arranged before CO)
- [ ] Prepare draft Deed of Easement and Restriction for attorney review
- [ ] Confirm 30-year affordability period language in all agreements

**RSIS Site Design**
- [ ] Street widths and design meet RSIS N.J.A.C. 5:21-4 standards
- [ ] Parking counts meet RSIS minimums; confirm whether local reduction for affordable units applies
- [ ] Stormwater management design initiated
- [ ] Pedestrian access routes and curb ramps included
- [ ] Utility plans: underground utilities required; easements identified

---

### Construction Documents / Permitting Phase

**NJ UCC Building Permit**
- [ ] Construction permit application submitted to municipality
- [ ] All subcodes covered: building, electrical, plumbing, mechanical, fire protection, energy, accessibility
- [ ] Energy compliance documentation: IECC compliance path selected (prescriptive, performance, or ENERGY STAR)
- [ ] ENERGY STAR pre-construction design review completed (LIHTC projects)

**Accessibility Documentation**
- [ ] Floor plans annotated with accessibility compliance:
  - Clear floor spaces at each fixture
  - Maneuvering clearances at all doors
  - Accessible route path
  - Reach range compliance for all controls
  - Grab bar blocking locations in all bathrooms
- [ ] ANSI A117.1-2017 technical specifications confirmed for Type A units
- [ ] Type B unit compliance confirmed for all FHA-covered units
- [ ] COAH townhouse requirements: adaptable entrance, first-floor bath/bedroom documented

**Site Permits**
- [ ] RSIS site plan approval from municipality
- [ ] DEP Stormwater permit (if applicable)
- [ ] DEP NJPDES permit (if ≥1 acre disturbed)
- [ ] DEP freshwater wetlands permit (if applicable)
- [ ] Municipal site plan approval obtained

---

### Construction Phase

**Sequencing (UHAC 2024 Phasing Rules)**
- [ ] Construction schedule reviewed against UHAC phasing thresholds
- [ ] Confirm: no more than 10% of market-rate units receive CO before first affordable unit CO
- [ ] Track market-rate CO count against affordable unit CO count throughout construction
- [ ] Construction loan draw schedule aligned with UHAC phasing rules
- [ ] Lender and title company notified of phasing requirements

**Inspections**
- [ ] ENERGY STAR mid-construction inspection scheduled (insulation, air sealing, framing)
- [ ] NJ UCC inspections: foundation, framing, rough-in, insulation, final
- [ ] Document all inspections in project file
- [ ] NJHMFA construction draws tied to inspection milestones

**Accessibility During Construction**
- [ ] Grab bar blocking verified during framing inspection
- [ ] Door rough openings verified before drywall (allow for 32″ clear after jamb installation)
- [ ] Shower/tub rough-in at correct height for transfer bench use
- [ ] Type A unit kitchen rough-in confirmed for removable base cabinet layout

---

### Pre-Occupancy / Close-Out Phase

**NJ UCC Certificate of Occupancy**
- [ ] Final building inspection completed
- [ ] Final electrical, plumbing, mechanical inspections completed
- [ ] Accessibility final inspection: confirm all required features installed
- [ ] CO issued by municipality

**ENERGY STAR (LIHTC)**
- [ ] ENERGY STAR final performance testing: blower door, duct leakage testing
- [ ] ENERGY STAR certification issued before CO request submitted to NJHMFA
- [ ] Certification submitted to NJHMFA for LIHTC compliance

**UHAC Affordability Controls**
- [ ] Deed of Easement and Restriction recorded with county
- [ ] Administrative Agent Agreement executed and recorded
- [ ] Affirmative Marketing Plan approved by NJHMFA / municipality
- [ ] Income limits and maximum rents calculated and documented (with utility allowances)
- [ ] Marketing period begun at least 90–120 days before occupancy

**Section 504 (Federally Funded)**
- [ ] Type A mobility-accessible units certified complete
- [ ] Sensory-accessible units (visual alarms, notification) inspected
- [ ] HUD-required certifications submitted to HUD or state agency

---

### Post-Occupancy / Ongoing Compliance

**Annual UHAC Obligations**
- [ ] Annual income recertification for all tenants (within 120 days of lease anniversary)
- [ ] Annual rent roll submitted to Administrative Agent and municipality
- [ ] Annual report to NJHMFA (if NJHMFA-funded)
- [ ] Rent increase calculated: not to exceed 2% annually; notify tenants

**LIHTC Annual Compliance**
- [ ] Annual tenant income certifications completed and filed
- [ ] Vacant unit rule: advertise and rent to income-eligible household
- [ ] Over-income tenant: "next-available-unit" rule enforced
- [ ] Form 8609 filed with IRS; retain all compliance records
- [ ] Annual monitoring fee paid to NJHMFA

**Physical Inspections**
- [ ] NJHMFA physical inspection every 3 years minimum
- [ ] REAC inspection (if project-based Section 8 / HUD-assisted)

## Common Compliance Pitfalls

### 1. FHA Door Clearance — Maneuvering Space
**Issue**: Only checking 32″ clear door width; missing latch-side maneuvering clearance.
**Rule**: FHA Requirement 3 + ANSI A117.1-2017 §404.2.4 requires clearance beyond the door width — typically 18″ on pull side, 12″ on push side.
**Prevention**: Detail maneuvering clearances on all unit entry and bathroom door sheets.

### 2. UHAC 2024 Phasing — Overlooked in Contracts
**Issue**: Pre-2024 construction contracts and loan agreements don't reflect December 19, 2024 UHAC phasing thresholds.
**Rule**: Max 10% market-rate before first affordable CO; max 25%+1 market-rate before 25% affordable.
**Prevention**: Update all construction contracts, lender agreements, and CO delivery schedules to reflect 2024 UHAC phasing rules. Review with construction counsel.

### 3. Section 504 Unit Count Rounding
**Issue**: Rounding 5% down instead of up; missing the minimum-1-unit floor.
**Example error**: 15 units × 5% = 0.75 → incorrectly rounded to 0. Correct answer = 1 unit.
**Prevention**: Always round up; minimum is 1 mobility unit + 1 sensory unit regardless of building size.

### 4. COAH Townhouse — Conversion Fund Missed
**Issue**: Architect designs adaptable townhouse entrance but developer omits the 10% conversion fund deposit.
**Rule**: For COAH-credited multistory townhouses, builder must deposit funds for future conversion of adaptable entrances to full accessibility for 10% of units.
**Prevention**: Include conversion fund escrow in project budget and developer agreement.

### 5. ENERGY STAR — Late Engagement of HERS Rater
**Issue**: HERS Rater engaged at construction rather than design, causing redesign costs or certification failure.
**Prevention**: Engage HERS Rater at schematic design phase. ENERGY STAR requires pre-construction design review; changes after framing are costly.

### 6. Utility Allowance Not Deducted from Rent
**Issue**: Maximum UHAC rents calculated without utility allowance deduction when tenant pays utilities.
**Rule**: If tenant pays any utilities, the appropriate HUD or housing authority utility allowance must be deducted from the maximum allowable rent.
**Prevention**: Confirm utility payment responsibility in leases; obtain current utility allowance schedule from county housing authority.

### 7. Grab Bar Backing — Insufficient Size or Location
**Issue**: Backing installed in correct general area but too small or at wrong height to support grab bars at all ANSI-required locations.
**Rule**: ANSI A117.1 and FHA Requirement 6 require reinforcement to support 250 lbs. at all future grab bar locations: at tub sides, tub ends, shower walls, and toilet side + rear walls.
**Prevention**: Detail grab bar blocking locations specifically on all bathroom elevation drawings with dimensions.

### 8. Bedroom Distribution — Wrong Mix
**Issue**: Developer provides required number of affordable units but wrong bedroom distribution.
**Rule**: UHAC and the municipal FAIR SHARE PLAN specify bedroom distribution. Studios/efficiencies typically capped at 10%; 3-bedroom minimum required.
**Prevention**: Confirm bedroom distribution with municipal planning department and in UHAC administrative agent agreement before locking in floor plan.

### 9. Deed Restriction Language Not Pre-Approved
**Issue**: Developer uses generic deed restriction language not accepted by NJHMFA or municipality.
**Prevention**: Use NJHMFA-approved deed restriction template; have reviewed by both NJHMFA and municipal attorney before recording. Corrections after recording are expensive.

### 10. Affirmative Marketing — Too Late, Too Narrow
**Issue**: Marketing begins 30 days before occupancy; reaches only general public; no outreach to targeted communities.
**Rule**: AFHMP must be approved before marketing begins; marketing must target underserved populations through specific media.
**Prevention**: Draft AFHMP at pre-construction phase; begin outreach 90–120 days before unit availability; document all outreach with dates and media receipts.

## Regulatory Overlap Summary

| Scenario | Applies |
|---|---|
| All NJ construction | NJ UCC, RSIS |
| 4+ unit building (private) | FHA 7 requirements (Type B) |
| 4+ unit with federal HUD funding | FHA + Section 504 (5%/2% Type A) |
| Affordable/COAH units | UHAC + COAH rules |
| LIHTC funding | UHAC + NJHMFA QAP + ENERGY STAR + LIHTC compliance |
| HOME funding | UHAC + HOME program rules + FHA |
| Government-owned housing | ADA Title II + Section 504 + FHA |
| COAH townhouses | Type A adaptable features + conversion fund |
| NJ tenant LAD protection | NJ LAD (source of income, broader protected classes) |
