# NJHMFA / LIHTC Compliance Review Skill

Review architectural drawings and project documentation for compliance with NJHMFA Design Standards, LIHTC program requirements, Fair Housing Act (FHA), ADA/Section 504, and applicable New Jersey building codes for affordable multifamily housing.

## Scope

This skill applies to:
- LIHTC (9% and 4% tax credit) developments financed through NJHMFA
- HOME-funded projects with NJHMFA as the allocating agency
- Mixed-income projects with NJHMFA financing
- Tax-exempt bond projects (4% credits) under NJHMFA

Trigger this skill whenever a user uploads, describes, or references drawings, specs, or narratives for an NJ affordable housing project, or asks about NJHMFA design standards or LIHTC compliance.

---

## Critical Rules

1. **Always check the current NJHMFA QAP year** – point values, set-aside requirements, and design standards update annually. Ask the user which QAP cycle applies if not stated.
2. **Unit mix drives tax credit calculation** – errors in bedroom count, gross square footage, or AMI targeting invalidate the credit amount.
3. **All ground-floor units must comply with FHA Type B minimum** – verify on every floor plan review.
4. **Section 504 / ADA accessible unit count must be calculated per building, not per project** – a common mistake that causes HUD findings.
5. **Never approve a parking ratio or unit count without verifying the current NJHMFA design standard** – minimums changed in recent QAP cycles.

## Compliance Checklist by Drawing Set

### A. Site Plan

- [ ] **Accessible parking** – 2% of total spaces (min 1) van-accessible, located on shortest accessible route to building entrance
- [ ] **Accessible route** – continuous, slope ≤ 1:20 (≤ 1:12 for ramps with handrails), width ≥ 44"
- [ ] **On-site amenity** – NJHMFA requires a minimum outdoor gathering/recreation area; confirm dimensions and surface material
- [ ] **Parking count** – meets local zoning minimum AND NJHMFA minimums (typically 1.0 space/unit for senior; 1.5 space/unit for family; verify current QAP)
- [ ] **Drop-off zone** – accessible passenger loading zone if project serves elderly/disabled population
- [ ] **Trash enclosure** – accessible approach path and clear floor space at dumpster/compactor
- [ ] **Bicycle parking** – NJHMFA Green Building Standards require covered bike parking; verify count (typically 1 per unit or per local ordinance)

### B. Floor Plans — Unit Mix & Square Footage

Cross-check every unit type against the **NJHMFA Minimum Net Square Footage** table (verify against current Design Standards document):

| Unit Type | NJHMFA Min Net SF (typical) |
|-----------|----------------------------|
| Studio / 0BR | 450 sf |
| 1 BR | 650 sf |
| 2 BR | 850 sf |
| 3 BR | 1,050 sf |
| 4 BR | 1,200 sf |

- [ ] **Confirm net vs. gross** – NJHMFA measures net habitable area; exclude mechanical chases, structural walls, and common corridors
- [ ] **Kitchen** – min 30" clearance between opposing counters (36" preferred for accessible units); dishwasher rough-in required for 3BR+
- [ ] **Bathroom** – min 5'×8' clear floor plan for Type B units; 5'×8' turning radius (60" diameter) for Type A/fully accessible units
- [ ] **Closet depths** – min 24" depth per NJHMFA standards
- [ ] **Bedroom dimensions** – min 100 sf per bedroom; 10' min dimension
- [ ] **Unit count matches LIHTC application** – any change from the approved LIHTC application must be reported to NJHMFA immediately

### C. Floor Plans — Accessibility

#### Fair Housing Act (FHA) Type B — All Ground-Floor Units and All Units in Elevator Buildings

- [ ] At least one accessible building entrance on an accessible route
- [ ] All common-use areas accessible and on accessible route
- [ ] Doors: 32" clear (36" nominal) at primary entry; 32" clear at all interior doors (28" min nominal for secondary)
- [ ] Reinforced walls in all bathrooms for future grab bar installation
- [ ] Usable kitchen: clear floor space ≥ 30"×48" at each fixture; accessible route through kitchen
- [ ] Usable bathroom: clear floor space 30"×48" at fixtures; turning space (60" diameter or T-turn)
- [ ] Light switches, outlets, and thermostats between 15" and 48" AFF (forward reach) or 9"–54" AFF (side reach)

#### Section 504 / ADA Accessible Units (Federally Assisted Projects)

Calculate required accessible unit counts per 24 CFR Part 8 and HUD Notice H-2013-02:
- **5% of total units** fully accessible (mobility) — at least 1 unit
- **2% of total units** accessible for hearing/vision impaired — at least 1 unit
- Units must be **distributed across bedroom sizes and rent levels** proportionally
- Fully accessible units must be **on an accessible route** from parking/drop-off

For each fully accessible unit, verify:
- [ ] 60" turning radius in bathroom, bedroom approach, and kitchen
- [ ] Roll-in shower or accessible tub (per design intent)
- [ ] 36" clear width on primary circulation path within unit
- [ ] Lowered countertops or knee clearance per ADA Standards §804 / §603
- [ ] Visual alarms in all rooms

#### NJHMFA Universal Design Requirements (verify current QAP for point category)

- [ ] Lever door hardware throughout
- [ ] Rocker switches
- [ ] Offset hinges or wider door frames
- [ ] Blocking for grab bars in all bathrooms (not just accessible units)
- [ ] Step-free shower with fold-down seat in accessible units
- [ ] Contrasting color thresholds

### D. Common Areas & Amenity Spaces

- [ ] **Community room** – required for family projects ≥ 12 units; min area per NJHMFA (typically 1,000 sf or 15 sf/unit, whichever is greater — verify)
- [ ] **Management office** – min 150 sf, accessible, on ground floor or served by elevator
- [ ] **Laundry room** – accessible machines and clear floor space; one washer and dryer per 12 units (or per local ordinance); accessible machines at required ratio
- [ ] **Mail area** – accessible reach range for all boxes; USPS clearances
- [ ] **Fitness room / computer lab** – if provided for QAP points, confirm minimum equipment list per current QAP
- [ ] **Elevator** – required for buildings ≥ 4 stories; NJHMFA recommends for all buildings ≥ 3 stories serving elderly/mobility-impaired residents; cab min 68"×51" clear (ADA-compliant)

### E. Building Envelope & Energy Standards

NJHMFA requires compliance with **one** of the following (confirm with current QAP):
- Enterprise Green Communities Criteria (current version)
- ENERGY STAR Multifamily New Construction (ESMFNC)
- LEED for Homes Multifamily or LEED BD+C (Silver or higher)
- NJ HMFA Green Building Requirements (standalone)

- [ ] **Insulation** – meets or exceeds IECC 2021 prescriptive values for NJ Climate Zone 4A (walls, roof, slab, basement)
- [ ] **Windows** – max U-0.30 and max SHGC 0.40 for CZ4A (verify against current IECC/ENERGY STAR spec)
- [ ] **Air sealing** – target ≤ 3 ACH50; blower door test required at completion
- [ ] **Mechanical ventilation** – balanced or exhaust-only ventilation per ASHRAE 62.2 for each unit; no shared exhaust
- [ ] **HVAC efficiency** – ENERGY STAR rated equipment specified (SEER ≥ 15 / HSPF ≥ 8.8 for split systems; EF for water heaters)
- [ ] **Healthy homes** – no carpet in kitchens/baths; low-VOC paints and adhesives spec'd; CO detector in every unit with fossil fuel appliances
- [ ] **Radon** – NJ is predominantly Zone 1; confirm radon-resistant construction or mitigation per EPA guidelines

### F. Fire, Life Safety & Building Code

- [ ] **Fire sprinklers** – NFPA 13 or 13R required for buildings ≥ 4 stories (verify local fire official requirements for ≤ 3 stories); NJHMFA strongly recommends for all new construction
- [ ] **Fire alarm** – addressable system with visual alarms in all units per NFPA 72 and IBC/IFC
- [ ] **Egress** – two means of egress from every floor; egress paths ≥ 44" wide; stair handrails per NJ UCC R311/IBC 1011
- [ ] **Exit lighting and signage** – emergency illumination and exit signs at all egress points
- [ ] **Occupancy separation** – confirm correct occupancy classification (typically R-2 for multifamily; confirm if community room pushes to A-3)
- [ ] **NJ Uniform Construction Code** – adopted IBC/IRC edition with NJ amendments; confirm edition with AHJ

## LIHTC Compliance — Program-Level Checks

### Unit Mix vs. LIHTC Application / LURA

Compare the architectural drawings directly to the approved LIHTC application and Land Use Restriction Agreement (LURA):

- [ ] **Total unit count** matches LURA
- [ ] **Bedroom mix** matches LURA (e.g., 20 one-BR at 50% AMI, 40 two-BR at 60% AMI)
- [ ] **Unit square footage** — any reduction from application values may require NJHMFA approval and IRS notification
- [ ] **Common areas** — square footage of common areas is not included in eligible basis; confirm allocation between residential and common use
- [ ] **Accessible unit locations** — accessible unit building/floor must match what was represented in the application for housing choice voucher/Section 8 integration

### Eligible Basis & Eligible Basis Boost

- [ ] **QCT / DDA boost** – if project is in a Qualified Census Tract or Difficult Development Area, confirm with current IRS list (published annually); eligible basis may be boosted up to 130%
- [ ] **Historic tax credit combination** – if layering HTCs, confirm developer fee limits and basis allocation with syndicator and tax counsel
- [ ] **Developer fee cap** – NJHMFA caps developer fee in basis; confirm fee does not exceed current cap (verify QAP)
- [ ] **Contractor profit/overhead/general requirements** – confirm within NJHMFA limits

### Placed-in-Service & Carryover Milestones

- [ ] **10% test** – must expend ≥ 10% of reasonably expected basis within 12 months of allocation (for carryover projects); confirm with owner's tax counsel
- [ ] **Placed-in-Service date** – confirm construction schedule allows units to be placed in service by Dec 31 of the credit year (or by the extended deadline in the carryover agreement)

## Common Errors & How to Catch Them

| Error | How to Catch |
|-------|-------------|
| Unit SF below NJHMFA minimum | Dimension every room on plan; calculate net habitable area excluding walls and chases |
| Accessible route breaks at grade change | Follow the accessible route on site plan continuously; flag any grade change > 2% without a ramp or lift |
| FHA Type B door clearances insufficient | Mark up every door on floor plan with measured clear width; 32" clear = 34" nominal with 1¾" door |
| Accessible unit count wrong | Re-calculate: round UP for 5% rule; distribute by bedroom type using project unit mix table |
| Parking below NJHMFA minimum | Check local zoning AND NJHMFA minimum; project must meet higher of the two |
| Community room too small | Compute: max(1,000 sf, 15 sf × total unit count); check plans against this value |
| Missing visual alarms in accessible units | Mark accessible units on each floor plan; confirm visual alarm symbol in all rooms |
| HVAC equipment not ENERGY STAR rated | Cross-reference mechanical schedule with current ENERGY STAR product lists |

## Workflow

Make a todo list for all tasks and work through them in order.

### 1. Gather Documents

Ask the user for:
- Current NJHMFA QAP year and any project-specific design waivers granted
- Complete drawing set (site plan, all floor plans, elevations, sections, details)
- LIHTC application / LURA / carryover allocation letter
- Mechanical/electrical schedules or specifications (if available)
- Any prior NJHMFA design review comments

### 2. Identify Project Parameters

Extract and confirm:
- Total unit count and bedroom mix
- AMI targeting per bedroom type
- Number of buildings and stories
- Elevator vs. walk-up
- Presence of federal assistance (HUD financing triggers Section 504)
- QCT/DDA status
- Green building path (Enterprise Green Communities, ENERGY STAR, LEED, or NJHMFA standalone)

### 3. Run Compliance Checklists

Work through each checklist section (A through F above) systematically. For each item:
- Mark Pass / Fail / N/A
- Note drawing sheet and detail number where the condition is visible
- Flag items that require waiver or additional documentation

### 4. Unit-by-Unit SF Audit

For every unit type, measure net habitable area from the floor plan:
1. Sum room areas (bedroom, living, dining, kitchen, bathrooms, closets, hallways within unit)
2. Subtract structural wall area (estimate at 5–8% for wood frame; 3–5% for masonry CMU)
3. Compare to NJHMFA minimum for that unit type
4. Flag units below minimum; note deficiency in sf

### 5. Accessible Route Trace

On the site plan and each floor plan:
1. Identify all accessible parking spaces and building entrances
2. Trace the accessible route from each parking space to each elevator and to at least one ground-floor accessible unit
3. Verify no gaps, lips, stairs, or cross-slopes > 2%
4. Verify all door clearances along route

### 6. Compile Findings Report

Produce a findings report with:
- **Project name, address, QAP year**
- **Summary table**: total items checked, pass, fail, N/A
- **Detailed findings list**: sheet reference, description of deficiency, applicable standard, recommended corrective action
- **Priority flags**: items that will cause NJHMFA rejection or IRS recapture risk (label CRITICAL)
- **Waiver items**: items that may qualify for NJHMFA design waiver

### 7. Present Results

Present findings grouped by:
1. CRITICAL (will block financing or cause recapture)
2. REQUIRED CORRECTION (code/standard violation requiring redesign)
3. RECOMMENDED (best practice; not a hard requirement)
4. CONFIRM WITH OWNER/COUNSEL (program-level items needing legal or owner input)

## Wrap Up

After completing the review, provide the user with:

1. **Compliance Summary**: one-paragraph narrative suitable for inclusion in a NJHMFA design review submission letter
2. **Findings Log**: table with columns — Drawing Sheet | Checklist Item | Standard | Finding (Pass/Fail/N/A) | Corrective Action
3. **CRITICAL items list**: bulleted, with specific sheet/detail references
4. **Next steps**: list of outstanding documents needed, waivers to request, and resubmission triggers
