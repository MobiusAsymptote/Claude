# UHAC and COAH / Fair Share Housing Reference

## Uniform Housing Affordability Controls (UHAC)
### Citation: N.J.A.C. 5:80-26

### Administering Agency
- **New Jersey Housing and Mortgage Finance Agency (NJHMFA)**, under DCA
- Web: https://www.nj.gov/dca/hmfa/about/uhac/
- Full regulatory text: https://www.nj.gov/dca/hmfa/about/regulations/
- 2025 FAQ: https://www.nj.gov/dca/hmfa/about/regulations/docs/UHAC_FAQ.pdf

### Current Version
- **Substantially amended December 19, 2024** — first major overhaul since December 2004
- Prior to 2024 amendment, phasing rules were minimally regulated

### Scope
UHAC governs the affordability controls for all income-restricted housing units in New Jersey, including:
- COAH-credited inclusionary units
- Units required by Mount Laurel court orders
- Units funded by NJHMFA programs (LIHTC, HOME, AHPF, AHGS)
- Municipally mandated affordable units in any residential development

## Income Limits and Tiers

| Tier | AMI Threshold | Typical Programs |
|---|---|---|
| Very Low Income | ≤30% AMI | Section 8 project-based; some HOME units |
| Low Income | ≤50% AMI | LIHTC, HOME, COAH |
| Moderate Income | ≤80% AMI | LIHTC, COAH inclusionary |

- AMI = Area Median Income; updated annually by HUD by county/metro area
- Income limits published by NJ DCA annually for each county

---

## Affordability Period

| Housing Type | Minimum Duration |
|---|---|
| Rental | **30 years** from certificate of occupancy |
| For-sale (ownership) | **30 years** from initial sale |

- Deed restriction must be recorded in county land records
- Deed restriction language must include NJHMFA/COAH-approved template
- Annual monitoring by municipality or Administrative Agent required for duration

---

## Rent and Price Controls

### Maximum Rents
- Maximum affordable rents calculated as percentage of AMI for unit size
- Formula: (AMI × income tier %) × 30% ÷ 12 = max monthly rent
- Includes utility allowance deduction when tenant pays utilities
- Utility allowances: published by NJ DCA or local housing authority

### Annual Rent Adjustments
- Maximum annual increase: **2%** or CPI, per regulation
- Landlords must notify tenants and administrative agent before increases
- No rent increase permitted without updated income recertification

### For-Sale Maximum Prices
- Calculated based on 28% of gross monthly income for target income tier
- Must assume standard mortgage terms (30-year fixed; prevailing rate)
- Cannot include non-standard amenities in base price

## Phasing Requirements (2024 Amendment — KEY CHANGE)

These rules are new as of December 19, 2024 and apply to mixed-income inclusionary developments:

| Threshold | Rule |
|---|---|
| Maximum market-rate units before first affordable unit | **10%** of total market-rate units |
| Maximum market-rate units before 25% of affordable units | **25% + 1** of total market-rate units |
| Completion of all units | All affordable units complete before final market-rate CO |
| Feasibility exception | Available; requires NJHMFA approval |

**Practical impact**: Construction sequencing and lender draw schedules must be restructured to comply. Review all existing construction loan covenants against 2024 UHAC.

## Unit Mix Requirements

UHAC and municipal Fair Share Plans specify the required distribution of bedroom sizes among affordable units:

| Bedroom Count | Typical Required Share |
|---|---|
| Studio / efficiency | Up to 10% |
| 1-bedroom | 20–30% |
| 2-bedroom | 30–40% |
| 3-bedroom | 20–30% |
| 4-bedroom | Per municipal plan |

Exact percentages set in each municipality's Court-approved or DCA-approved Housing Element and Fair Share Plan.

## Marketing and Tenant Selection

- **Affirmative Marketing Plan (AMP)** required before leasing/selling
- AMP must reach historically underserved populations
- Advertising in targeted media (Spanish-language press, community organizations, etc.)
- **Lottery required** if number of applicants exceeds available units
- Preference points allowed for: municipal residents, people with disabilities, seniors
- Documentation of outreach must be maintained by Administrative Agent

---

## Administrative Agent Requirements

All UHAC-controlled developments must designate an Administrative Agent:
- Can be municipality, housing authority, non-profit, or private management company
- Must be approved by municipality and NJHMFA
- Responsibilities: income certification, marketing, lottery, annual recertification, monitoring
- Administrative Agent Agreement must be recorded before CO

## Ongoing Compliance Obligations

| Obligation | Frequency |
|---|---|
| Tenant income recertification | Annual |
| Rent roll submission to municipality | Annual |
| Report to NJHMFA / Administrative Agent | Annual |
| Physical inspection | Per loan agreement; minimum every 3 years |

## COAH Rules and Mount Laurel Fair Share Obligations

### Citation and Status
- N.J.A.C. 5:93 (Second Round rules) — still valid for municipalities with substantive certifications
- N.J.A.C. 5:97 (Third Round) — superseded by court and legislative action
- N.J.A.C. 5:99 (current) — DCA/DLPS administers ongoing fair share compliance
- N.J.S.A. 52:27D-301 et seq. (Fair Housing Act) — statutory basis

### Administering Agency
- **NJ DCA, Division of Local Planning Services (DLPS)** (COAH dissolved 2011; functions absorbed by DCA)
- Web: https://www.nj.gov/dca/dlps/
- Historical substantive certifications still valid for some municipalities

### Mount Laurel Doctrine
- Origin: NJ Supreme Court decisions (1975, 1983) — Southern Burlington County NAACP v. Mt. Laurel
- Constitutional obligation: every NJ municipality must use zoning powers affirmatively to accommodate its fair share of regional affordable housing need
- Enforced through NJ Superior Court (Law Division); municipalities that fail to comply subject to "builder's remedy" lawsuits

### Fourth Round Legislation (March 2024)
- Governor Murphy signed landmark affordable housing legislation
- Municipalities must adopt Housing Elements and Fair Share Plans every 10 years
- **84,000 additional affordable units** mandated statewide
- New DCA methodology for calculating municipal obligations
- Fourth Round methodology: https://nj.gov/dca/dlps/pdf/FourthRoundCalculation_Methodology.pdf

### Fair Share Income Distribution Requirements

| Tier | AMI | Required Minimum |
|---|---|---|
| Very Low Income | ≤30% AMI | **13%** of all affordable units |
| Low + Very Low combined | ≤50% AMI | **50%** of all affordable units |
| Moderate Income | ≤80% AMI | Remaining 50% |

### Accessory Dwelling Units (ADUs)
- Count toward municipal fair share obligations if income-restricted
- ADU units governed by UHAC if deed-restricted

### Development Fee Obligations (N.J.A.C. 5:97-6 / 5:93-8)
- Market-rate residential developments must pay an affordable housing development fee to the municipality
- Fee typically 1.5% of equalized assessed value (residential) or 2.5% (non-residential)
- Funds collected in Municipal Affordable Housing Trust Fund
- Trust funds used to finance affordable unit production

## COAH Type A Townhouse Requirements

For COAH-credited multistory townhouse units (split-level or stacked):

| Feature | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Entrance | Adaptable entrance; must have accessible entrance plan on file |
| First-floor bathroom | Adaptable; reinforced walls; roll-in or transfer shower if required |
| First-floor bedroom | Accessible bedroom on ground floor |
| 10% Conversion Fund | Builder must deposit funds sufficient to convert 10% of adaptable entrances to full accessibility |

Standard: ANSI A117.1 Type A dwelling unit features apply.

## Key Cross-References

- **UHAC + RSIS**: Both apply to all NJ affordable housing. RSIS governs the site; UHAC governs the units.
- **UHAC + NJ UCC**: NJ UCC sets construction standards; UHAC sets affordability standards. Both operate simultaneously.
- **UHAC + NJHMFA**: NJHMFA programs (LIHTC, HOME) layer additional requirements on top of UHAC baseline.
- **UHAC + FHA 7 Requirements**: Fair Housing Act accessibility requirements apply independently of UHAC affordability controls.
