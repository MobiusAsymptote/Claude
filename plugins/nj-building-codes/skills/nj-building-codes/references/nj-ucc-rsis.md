# NJ UCC and RSIS Reference

## NJ Uniform Construction Code (N.J.A.C. 5:23)

### Administering Agency
- **New Jersey Department of Community Affairs (DCA), Division of Codes and Standards**
- Address: 101 South Broad Street, PO Box 802, Trenton, NJ 08625-0802
- Phone: (609) 292-7899
- Web: https://www.nj.gov/dca/codes/
- Code text (online only): https://www.nj.gov/dca/codes/codreg/ucc.shtml

### Current Edition
- Base: **2021 International Building Code (IBC)** with NJ amendments
- Energy: Current IECC adoption
- Electrical: 2023 NEC (in proposed 2024–2025 update)
- Accessibility reference standard: **ANSI A117.1-2017**
- Print copies discontinued June 6, 2023; all code text online only

### Proposed 2024–2025 Update (Under Development)
Incorporates: 2024 IBC, 2024 IRC, 2024 IECC, 2024 IMC, 2024 IFGC, 2024 NSPC, 2023 NEC

### Chapter 11 — Accessibility
- References ICC A117.1-2017 for all technical specifications
- Buildings with elevators: all units must comply
- Buildings without elevators: ground-floor units must comply
- 4+ unit buildings subject to Fair Housing Act requirements (see accessibility reference)
- Accessible routes, common areas, door widths, unit interiors

### Chapter 13 / IECC — Energy
- Mandatory energy efficiency for all residential construction
- NJHMFA LIHTC projects must additionally obtain ENERGY STAR certification

### Permit and Inspection Process
- Building permit required before construction
- Construction Official and code enforcement at municipal level
- DCA enforces statewide minimums; municipalities may not adopt less stringent standards
- Certificate of Occupancy (CO) issued after final inspection
- Uniform fee schedule: N.J.A.C. 5:23-4.20

### Subcode Structure
| Subcode | Governing Document |
|---|---|
| Building | IBC + NJ amendments |
| Residential (1-2 family) | IRC + NJ amendments |
| Electrical | NEC + NJ amendments |
| Plumbing | NSPC + NJ amendments |
| Mechanical | IMC + NJ amendments |
| Fire Protection | IFC + NFPA + NJ amendments |
| Energy | IECC + NJ amendments |
| Accessibility | IBC Ch. 11 + ANSI A117.1-2017 |

## Residential Site Improvement Standards (N.J.A.C. 5:21)

### Administering Agency
- **New Jersey Department of Community Affairs (DCA)**
- Web: https://www.nj.gov/dca/codes/offices/rsis.shtml
- Regulatory text: https://www.nj.gov/dca/codes/codreg/pdf_regs/njac_5_21.pdf
- Overseen by: Site Improvement Advisory Board (SIAB), 12-member body

### Current Edition
- Most recent readoption effective **March 30, 2021**
- Periodic amendments; check DCA website for interim changes

### Purpose
RSIS provides statewide uniform standards for site improvements on residential development sites, reducing inconsistent municipal requirements and unnecessary construction costs.

### Scope
Applies to all residential development requiring site plan approval or subdivision approval in NJ.

### Parking Requirements (N.J.A.C. 5:21-4.14)

| Residential Unit Type | Min. Spaces / Unit |
|---|---|
| Studio / efficiency | 1.5 |
| 1-bedroom | 1.5 |
| 2-bedroom | 1.5 |
| 3-bedroom | 2.0 |
| 4+ bedroom | 2.0 |

- Garages count toward required spaces
- Accessible van-accessible spaces: 1 per 25 total (ADA/FHA)
- Municipalities may not require more than RSIS minimums without variance process

---

### Water Supply (N.J.A.C. 5:21-5)
- Public water connection required where available within 200 ft of property
- Private well standards apply where public water not available
- Fire hydrant spacing: every 500 ft in multi-family; per fire code
- Minimum domestic service pressure: 20 psi at meter

### Sanitary Sewer (N.J.A.C. 5:21-6)
- Public sewer connection required where available within 200 ft
- Private septic (NJDEP standards) where public sewer unavailable
- Manholes at every change of direction or grade; max 400 ft spacing

### Stormwater Management (N.J.A.C. 5:21-7)
- Design storm: 100-year for major developments; 10-year for minor
- Detention basin required when runoff increases post-development
- Water quality treatment for first 1.25 inches of rainfall
- Coordination required with NJDEP Stormwater Management Rules (N.J.A.C. 7:8)

### Grading and Fill (N.J.A.C. 5:21-8)
- Finished grade must slope away from structures (min 2% for 10 ft)
- Erosion and sediment control plan required
- Coordination with DEP when disturbing ≥1 acre (NJPDES permit)

### Utilities (N.J.A.C. 5:21-9)
- Underground utility requirement for all new residential streets
- Street lighting: per IESNA standards
- Easements required for all utility infrastructure

### Pedestrian Access
- Sidewalks required in all developments of 5+ units
- Curb ramps (ADA-compliant) at all crossings
- ADA-compliant pedestrian access routes to common areas and amenities

---

### RSIS Variance Process
Municipalities or applicants may request SIAB review for deviations from RSIS standards. Standards cannot be made less stringent by local ordinance; variances granted only for site-specific practical difficulties.

## NJ UCC + RSIS Cross-Reference for Affordable Housing

| Project Phase | Applicable Standards |
|---|---|
| Site plan / subdivision | RSIS, Municipal zoning ordinance, DEP stormwater |
| Building permit application | NJ UCC, ADA, FHA (if 4+ units) |
| Construction | NJ UCC subcodes, ENERGY STAR (if LIHTC) |
| Certificate of Occupancy | NJ UCC final inspection; UHAC affordability controls recorded |
| Post-occupancy | UHAC annual monitoring; ENERGY STAR maintenance |
